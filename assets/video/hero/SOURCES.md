# Hero reel sources

Three renditions of the same cut, all scaled from the clips' 4K (3840x2160) originals with Lanczos and a light sharpen:
`reel-1440.mp4` (2560x1440, CRF 27, wide high-density screens), `reel-1080.mp4` (1920x1080, CRF 25, other desktops),
`reel-720.mp4` (1280x720, CRF 27, below 900 px). 9 clips, 7 s each (clip 9 is 5 s), 1 s cross-fades, unified grade, 54 s.
The reel ends on the two seconds that precede clip 1's in-point, so the loop is seamless.
